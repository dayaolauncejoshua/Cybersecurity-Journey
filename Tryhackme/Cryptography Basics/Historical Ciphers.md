**Historical Ciphers**

1. Caesar Cipher
    - The Caesar Cipher is one of the oldest and simplest encryption methods. It was used around the first century BCE and works by shifting each letter by a fixed number of positions in the alphabet.
    - Example
        * Plaintext: TRYHACKME
        * Key: 3
        * Cipher: Caesar Cipher
        * Shift: 3 letters to the right
        * Ciphertext: WUBKDFNPH
    - Plaintext + Caesar Cipher + Key (3) → Ciphertext
