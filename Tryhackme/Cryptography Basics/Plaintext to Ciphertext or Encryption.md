**Cipher**
    - The encryption function is part of the cipher. 
    - is an algorithm to convert plaintext to ciphertext and vice versa.
    - To recover the plaintext, we must pass the ciphertext along with the proper key via the decryption function, which would give us the original plaintext.

**Key terms:**
1. Plaintext is the original, human readable message, or the data before encryption. It could be a chat message, image, document, a multimedia file, etc.
2. Ciphertext is the scrambled, unreadable data after encryption.
3. Cipher is the algorithm/method used to encrypt and decrypt data. eg. AES is a cipher.
4. Key is a piece of data in bits that the cipher use to encrypt and decrpyt data.
    - In most encryption systems, the cipher is publicly known, but the secret key must be protected.
5. Encryption is the process of converting plaintext to ciphertext using a cipher and a key. A cipher can be known, but the secret key needs to be the one protected
6. Decryption is the reverce process of encryption. Converting encrypted data back to original/plaintext. Without the correct key, decryption is not feasible
